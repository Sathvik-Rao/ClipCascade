ClipCascade for macOS (Apple Silicon / Intel)
===========================================

A lightweight utility to sync clipboard content seamlessly across devices
with privacy and encryption.

Download the archive matching your Mac's processor:
- Apple Silicon (M1 or newer): ClipCascade-Apple_macOS.ARM_M-Series.zip
- Intel: ClipCascade-Apple_macOS.Intel-Series.zip

Install
-------
1. Unzip this archive ON macOS (zipping/unzipping on Windows strips
   macOS file attributes and Gatekeeper will refuse to launch the app).
2. Move ClipCascade.app to /Applications.
3. Right-click ClipCascade.app -> Open the first time (unsigned build).
4. ClipCascade runs as a menu-bar app (no dock icon by default).
